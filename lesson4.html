Saludos y presentaciones

Asking someone's name and saying your name

Introducing people
Te presento a...
Let me introduce ... to you.
Mucho gusto.
It's a pleasure.
Encantado.
Nice to meet you. (male)
Encantada.
Nice to meet you. (female)
Remember: Encantado is used by a male speaker and encantada by a female speaker.
